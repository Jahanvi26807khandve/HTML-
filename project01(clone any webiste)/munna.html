About The Movie

Munna Bhai M.B.B.S. is a Bollywood comedy-drama that follows Munna Bhai as he enters medical college and discovers that being a doctor is about more than just studying medicine. The movie combines comedy, friendship and an emotional story about humanity.

Movie Details

  • Release Year: 2003
  • Director: Rajkumar Hirani
  • Genre: Comedy - Drama
  • Language: Hindi
